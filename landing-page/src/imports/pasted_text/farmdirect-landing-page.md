Create a premium, production-quality landing page for **FarmDirect**, a modern agricultural marketplace that connects buyers directly with farmers and helps customers make more informed decisions about fresh produce.

The website should feel like a **premium Indian agritech startup**, combining the visual polish of a modern fintech/product website with the warmth and authenticity of agriculture.

## Brand

**Brand:** FarmDirect
**Tagline:** Fresh From Farms. Trusted By You.

Core idea:

**Farm → FarmDirect → Buyer**

FarmDirect helps people discover fresh agricultural products, understand where their food comes from, explore transparent product information, and use an AI-powered vegetable scanner to identify visible produce characteristics.

The website must communicate:

* Direct connection between farmers and buyers
* Fresh agricultural products
* Transparent marketplace experience
* AI-powered produce scanning
* Trust and transparency
* Modern technology supporting traditional agriculture

Do NOT make it look like a generic grocery store website.

---

# OVERALL DESIGN GOAL

The final result should look like a serious startup website that could be shown during a national-level hackathon, investor demo, college project presentation, or product launch.

Think:

**Agriculture + Apple-level product presentation + premium grocery commerce + modern AI**

The website should make someone immediately understand:

**FarmDirect connects farmers and buyers, adds transparency to fresh-produce shopping, and gives buyers an AI-powered way to explore produce.**

Prioritize visual storytelling, product screenshots, whitespace, strong typography, and premium interactions over large amounts of text.
